Professional Summary

Adeola Adewale, Ph.D is a seasoned Specialist with over eight years of experience managing complex projects across Africa. As Chief Technical Officer at Richflood LLC, I lead environmental and social studies, including ESIAs, climate risk assessments, and sustainability programs, ensuring compliance with both national and international standards. My work spans multiple sectors, like the energy, mining, agriculture, and infrastructural sector.

In addition to my technical work, I actively promote gender inclusion, child protection, and sustainable practices through initiatives such as Women in Mining Africa (WiM-Africa). I am committed to delivering impactful solutions that drive sustainable development, strengthen ESG compliance, and create positive environmental and social outcomes.
